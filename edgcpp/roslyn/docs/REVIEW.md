`Verified` refers to the listed tested scope, not full M0–M5 completion.

C++ and every other approved native extension remain in scope.

cheap metadata is retained; optional coordinate/range coverage and intricate
provenance recovery are not completion gates. Semantic identity/order, executable
behavior, types/lifetimes and actual tables/variables take priority.
